## Sole active choice

- **A — every supported edge has an independent witness:** `N_v=L_v`, or
  `I_v(p)=C_v(p)` for every exact pair. Every cell in every pair's footprint has
  some singleton witness. Coupled packages and coupled-only individual receipts
  or routes may still exist. This maximizes cell-level attribution but imposes
  the full edge-by-edge singleton matrix and invites rare token-shadow witnesses
  that exist only to certify an otherwise coupled edge.
- **B, recommended — every cell has an independent exemplar, with pair-specific
  coupled-only edges:** `N_v` is proper in `L_v`, while its cell projection is
  all of `C6`. Every cell has a singleton witness somewhere, every pair retains
  one under C3c.53-A, and at least one exact pair/cell edge exists only through
  coupled packages. This preserves a causal reference for every recipe language
  while retaining indispensable pair-specific cross-cell identity. Its cost is
  pair-keyed explanation complexity and a later disclosure question; token
  content could concentrate all six exemplars on one showcase pair.
- **C — at least one cell is globally coupled-only:** the cell projection of
  `N_v` is a nonempty proper subset of `C6`. At least one cell still appears in
  `L_v` under C3c.51-A but never has a singleton witness for any pair. Every pair
  receives some other singleton under C3c.53-A. This can make one or more cell
  languages intrinsically relational on the native-dual surface, but removes
  any independently attributable native-dual reference for them and risks a
  token or opaque “mystery cell.”
