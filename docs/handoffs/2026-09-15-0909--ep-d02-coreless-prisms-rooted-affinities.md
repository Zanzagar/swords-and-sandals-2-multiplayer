## Ternary frontier

```text
SINGLE-CORE AFFINITY              PLURAL-CORE AFFINITY
Fire    [CORE][a]                 Fire    [CORE 1][CORE 2][a]
Stone   [CORE][b]                 Stone   [CORE 1][CORE 2][b]
Void    [CORE][a]                 Void    [CORE 1][CORE 2][a]
        one fixed motif                    broader fixed spine
```

- **A, recommended — exactly one common anchor on every affinity key:**
  `R_{plural-core,v}` is empty. Every affinity exact key has `g=1`; every key
  still rotates at least one non-core label. This makes the archetype contrast
  sharp: prisms have no universal answer, while affinities have one enduring
  motif and let Souls author the surrounding composition. The cost is no
  broader invariant foundation and the danger that a badly tuned core becomes
  the only part players value.
- **B — mix single-core and plural-core affinity keys:** the plural set is
  nonempty and proper. At least one affinity key has `g=1` and another `g>=2`.
  This adds internal depth, but also another exact-snapshot taxonomy before
  stable ownership is settled. One obscure plural specimen can tokenize it.
  B requires at least two affinity keys plus the required prism key, hence at
  least three multi-anchor exact keys.
- **C — every affinity key has a plural core:** the plural set equals the
  affinity domain. Every affinity key has `g>=2`, creating a strong invariant
  ritual frame. The cost is that Soul response may feel like an accent around a
  predetermined Relic. Every affinity key then needs singleton count at least
  three and total cell degree at least four; compact two-anchor affinities are
  impossible.

Concrete single-core Ashen for Souls `F/S/V`:

`I_F=I_V={H/action,X/action}`

`I_S={H/action,H/build}`

Only `H/action` survives all Souls; `F/V` share a map and `S` rotates the other
label.

Concrete plural-core Tide:

`I_F=I_V={H/action,S/action,X/action}`

`I_S={H/action,S/action,S/build}`

`{H/action,S/action}` is the two-label core and the third label rotates. B uses
both examples; C requires every affinity key to follow Tide's plural pattern.
The coreless pair-complete Dreamglass remains the separate prism archetype.

A/B/C are exhaustive because the plural-core subset of the nonempty affinity
domain is empty, nonempty proper, or full. Zero-core affinity is excluded by
C67-A and is not a fourth option. Every affinity key obeys
`1<=g<k<d<=6`; single-core keys have `2<=k<=5` and `3<=d<=6`, while plural-
core keys have `2<=g<=4`, `3<=k<=5`, and `4<=d<=6`. All choices retain the
global supported-Soul range `3<=|S_v|<=15`.

Proof that `g=1` needs one universal label plus complete evidence that every
other candidate is absent under at least one supported Soul. Failed search is
not proof. Proof that `g>=2` needs two exact labels with valid singleton
packages under every supported Soul. Neither result proves ease, frequency,
visibility, utility, or power.
