### A — no locked definition is pair-complete

Every locked definition contains an unequal-family pair and at least one equal-
family pair. Each therefore needs at least three exact keys, such as
`F0,F1,F0`.

A's strongest case is layered expression: a family skeleton can recur while a
finer block assignment, predicate, instance state, or boundary changes. Its
costs are a three-key minimum, loss of family-as-form identity, and token risk
from repeating `M` through an irrelevant snapshot. Tight floors are
`6/10/60`.

### B — pair-complete and non-pair-complete names coexist

At least one locked definition gives every exact key a different family, while
another has both unequal- and equal-family pairs.

B supports clean relational forms beside layered names. Its costs are another
hidden subtype, the broadest comparison surface, and one token witness for
either half. It requires a two-key complete name and a distinct three-key
incomplete name, giving tight floors `7/12/72`.

### C — every locked definition is pair-complete — recommended

For every locked definition, the map from completed exact key to complete
family is injective. A new exact simulacrum always carries a new internal
relationship; it never repeats a family and differs only on a hidden finer
axis.

C gives the cleanest locked-class verb and retains `6/9/54`. Its cost is not
free: each stable tuple has finitely many legal families, every additional
instance/state/boundary key consumes one, and same-family assignment or
predicate variants become impossible within the locked class unless the rule
is reopened. The tight geometry may carry `F0/F1` or `F0/F1/F2`, but not a
fourth pair-distinct exact key.

A/B/C are exhaustive because the set of pair-complete locked definitions is
empty, nonempty proper, or the whole nonempty class. “Does every key
participate?” is not a fourth choice; it is already derived. Nor does C author
every legal family or select an exact family count.

All branches preserve Soul counts `{6,8,9,10,12,14,15}`, attain tight degree
five, and leave degree six legal. At one fixed exact-key/Soul-roster boundary,
fit remains direct. Every unequal-family same-definition pair has partial fit;
the empty/mixed/universal coverage topology is aggregate. C is recommended for
legibility and structural orthogonality, not because universal coverage makes
the separate-simulacrum relationship a direct analogy to *Achintya Bheda
Abheda*.
