## Semantic boundary

A3D1 asks what the participant may **author**, not how many final laws exist or
how they are displayed:

- A rich authored conditional covenant remains closed if selected whole.
- Material participant composition, ordering, filtering, weighting, or bounded
  parameterization of typed policy-bearing primitives is constructive.
- Presets and generated IDs do not erase a real builder; many dropdown values
  or a faceted UI do not manufacture one.
- No-op, cosmetic, duplicate, unreachable, zero-weight, or shadowed clauses do
  not establish construction after canonicalization.
- Every construct must terminate and complete to one total target law.

covenants whose collision and meaningful companion semantics move together.

## Highest-value next step

Ask for A3D1 A, B, C, or an explicit replacement:

- **A — closed authored whole covenants only. Recommended.** Every policy
  Relic offers a compact finite menu of indivisible bundled covenants such as
  Merciful Chorus and Defiant Dissonance. This preserves material orientation,
  artifact voice, forecastability, and multiplayer ownership with the smallest
  optimizer and migration surface. Its rationale fails if the menu becomes
  dozens of near-duplicate pseudo-primitives.

  template risk.

  the Loom while Oathbound Relics speak complete vows. This maximizes identity
  contrast and the risk that constructive Relics become the premium expert
  caste. It is a localized exception rather than the default fallback.

bloat fallback**. Fire B only if compact menus fail across representative
policy Relics through near-duplicate proliferation or omission of a recurring,

pass stronger sidegrade proof.
