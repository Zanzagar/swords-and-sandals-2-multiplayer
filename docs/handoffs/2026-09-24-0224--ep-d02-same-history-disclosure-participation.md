### A — every dual tag has a both-form disclosure history — recommended

`E^{pre-both-hist}_v=D^{dual}_v`. Every dual tag has at least one exact legal
history on which qualifying P and R accounts are each authorized before their
respective choices. The opportunities, pairs, references, roles, and players
may differ. Other histories may expose both forms, one, or neither.

At each `O^-` boundary its disclosed form is **direct**. Two accounts
participating within one continuing fixed-tag history are **partial** when
their boundaries differ. Excluding pure cross-history stitching is
**neutral/protective**, and universal tag participation is **aggregate**.

A most strongly supports one continuing Soul–Relic relationship making both
distinct causal faces intelligible within one lived temporal history. Its
costs are richer teaching/localization/validation per tag, less route-specific
mystery, and token compliance through one obscure both-form history.

Example: on one Ashen history, an earlier Heat choice exposes P and a later
Invocation choice exposes R. Dreamglass has an analogous history. The accounts
need not share an opportunity or player.

### B — every dual tag keeps its disclosed forms history-separated

`E^{pre-both-hist}_v` is empty. Each tag's nonempty P-history and R-history
sets are disjoint. C158-A therefore forces a P-supported history and a distinct
R-supported history for every tag. Other histories may expose neither form;
B does not say every history is single-form.

This is the strongest focused-initiation countercase: one biography teaches
bond repair and another teaches manifestation permission. Each positive account
is **direct**; same-tag continuity across histories is **partial**; the missing
form within each positive history is **neutral/unsupported**, while deliberate
teaching separation may be **neutral/protective**; universal separation is
**aggregate**.

Costs are that no actual recurrence history teaches the complete causal
vocabulary, players must stitch alternate biographies, and one form can hide
on a rare route.

Example: Ashen's Heatbound history exposes P and no R; its Invocation history
exposes R and no P; exhaustive analysis finds no Ashen history exposing both.

### C — same-history and history-separated dual tags coexist

`E^{pre-both-hist}_v` is a nonempty proper subset of `D^{dual}_v`. At least
one tag has a both-form history. At least one distinct tag has none and carries
its P and R accounts on different histories. Either class may retain opaque
residual histories.

This supports articulated covenants beside initiatory simulacra. The classes
inherit A/B's local ratings and their coexistence is **aggregate**. Costs are
another teaching subtype, likely clarity/flexibility premiums, validation
burden, and token positive membership.

Example: Ashen exposes P and R on one history. Dreamglass exposes P on one
history and R on another, with no Dreamglass history exposing both.
