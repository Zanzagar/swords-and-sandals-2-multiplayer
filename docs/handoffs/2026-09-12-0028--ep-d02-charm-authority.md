## Sole active choice

EP-D02-C3c.4 selects only which side a Charm definition may author:

- **A — input-grammar only:** every Charm may alter only the relationship
  predicate over later-eligible sources, never the payoff descriptor or
  magnitude.
- **B — payoff only:** every Charm leaves source membership and the complete
  pair-selected predicate unchanged and may alter only the qualitative payoff
  side; magnitude, payoff count, and cost remain later.
- **C, recommended — typed one-side catalog:** the schema supports both types
  and the complete design includes at least one materially functional example
  of each, but every individual Charm is statically exactly one type and cannot
  change both sides.

C preserves the richest collectible buildcraft without making one Charm a
mini-Legendary. Its cost is a larger UI/test matrix and a dangerous
easiest-input-plus-best-payoff package if later count/compatibility lets both
types coexist. Symbolically, an input Charm may change `S1 -> S2` to `S2 -> S1`
while keeping `P0`; a separate payoff Charm may change `P0` to `P1` while
keeping the predicate. One Charm cannot do both, and this choice neither permits
nor forbids those two Charms from being active together.

All options preserve the two-source minimum, later source eligibility, source
records/Load, material Bound-Soul/Relic variation, and the no-effect-triggered-
effect gate. They defer exact grammar/content/power, resonance precedence,
ownership/access, Relic/Charm counts and attachment, duplicates, budget,
acquisition, swap/rebinding timing, compatibility, and release.
