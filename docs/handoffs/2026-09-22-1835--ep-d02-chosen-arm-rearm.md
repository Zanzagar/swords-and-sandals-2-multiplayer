## Active owner choice — EP-D02-C3c.140

For legal adjacent-commit witness `w` and opportunity `d in D^{live}(w)`,
define **chosen-arm live-internal leverage** `L^{chosen}(w,d)` only when:

1. one arm of a C139-style isolation pair is the actual continuation selected
   at `d` in `w`;
2. the actual arm exactly reproduces `w`'s realized suffix through its `q2`;
   the counterfactual changes only the action at `d` and holds every later
   participant choice and exogenous/random input to that suffix wherever
   legally alignable; if alignment becomes illegal before the comparison
   resolves, the pair fails;
3. fixed `h` remains operative in both arms at every authoritative semantic
   cut through distinguishing cut `tau`;
4. at `tau`, the arms differ in a predicate-read `H`/`S` fact, tag-specific
   recurrence authorization, or both; and
5. replacing the selected continuation with the other isolated alternative
   changes whether, or at which later semantic cut, renewed sufficiency plus
   authorization produces `q2`.

The selected continuation need not solely, immediately, beneficially, or
completely rebuild the relationship. It may create, preserve, accelerate, or
delay a fact. A selected maintain, wait, or forbearance alternative qualifies
when substituting the other arm changes `q2`. A fused predicate-plus-gate
contrast counts once and proves neither axis independently. Context may be lost
after `tau`.

Let `F^{menu-only}_v` contain tags with at least one legal adjacent-commit
witness in which no live/false opportunity has `L^{chosen}`. C139-A guarantees
that such a witness still offers some unanchored live-internal lever; its actual
selected continuations simply use none of them. Negative membership requires
complete reachable-domain analysis.
