## Ternary frontier

For each multi-anchor exact realization `b`, define

`G_v(b)=intersection over s in S_v of I_v(b,s)`

and

`R_{core,v}={b in R_{multi,v} : G_v(b) is nonempty}`.

C3c.65 classifies all-Soul common-core prevalence:

- **A — universal cores:** `R_{core,v}=R_{multi,v}`. Every multi-anchor key has
  at least one singleton label present under every supported Soul.
- **B, recommended — mixed cores:** `R_{core,v}` is nonempty and proper. At
  least one key has a persistent identity spine and at least one is a coreless,
  pairwise-overlapping relational cycle.
- **C — no cores:** `R_{core,v}` is empty. Every multi-anchor key is pairwise
  continuous but lacks any anchor shared across the complete Soul roster.

A creates the cleanest “stable spine plus rotating remainder” grammar. Its cost
is less relational variety and a universal combinatorial restriction: on
C3c.63-B's required pair-complete key, `g>=1` shared core labels leave at most
`binom(d-g,k-g)<=10` distinct Soul maps within six cells. B retains clear
reference Relics and higher-mastery cyclic Relics without adding that global
ten-Soul ceiling; the cost is another structure to teach and token-showcase
risk. C maximizes relational identity but removes every permanent multi-anchor
reference and forces at least four supported Souls: with only three Souls, an
affinity key has exactly two distinct overlapping maps, whose intersection is
necessarily a common core.

A core-bearing pair-complete Prism can use
`I_F={S/action,X/action}`, `I_S={S/action,S/build}`, and
`I_V={S/action,X/build}`; `S/action` survives every Soul. A coreless pair-
complete Dreamglass can instead use `I_F={S/action,X/action}`,
`I_S={S/build,X/action}`, and `I_V={S/action,S/build}`; each pair overlaps but
the three-way intersection is empty. A core-bearing affinity Ashen can use
`I_F=I_V={H/action,X/action}` and `I_S={H/action,H/build}`. B requires both a
core-bearing and a coreless key without selecting their alignment to the pair-
complete and affinity classes.

A/B/C are exhaustive because `R_{core,v}` is empty, nonempty proper, or full.
Core size is a dependent refinement, not another option here. Proof of a
positive core needs one exact label singleton-backed under every supported
Soul. Proof of no core needs complete exact-set classification showing that
every candidate label is absent from `I_v` under at least one Soul. Pairwise
overlap witnesses alone cannot prove either result.
