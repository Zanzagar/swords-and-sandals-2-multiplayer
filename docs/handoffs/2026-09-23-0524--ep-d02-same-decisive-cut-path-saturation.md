### A — every dual history has a same-cut joint opportunity — recommended

`U^{all-same-chi}_v=D^{dual}_v`. Every legal actual history of every dual tag
contains at least one joint opportunity with intersecting predicate and
recurrence anchored-cut sets. Other joint opportunities on the same history
may remain cross-cut.

A closes C151-A's one-obscure-history loophole and makes a same-cut semantic
witness available on every legal dual rearm history. It does not guarantee a
player-visible beat; one dominated or mechanically obscure ritual per history
can still satisfy A. At each shared cut, continuing context and both independent
margins coexist **directly**; unequal downstream
effects and the larger recurrence remain **partial**; excluding cross-cut-only
paths is **neutral/protective**; and universal path-and-tag saturation is
**aggregate**. Costs are synchronized-boundary authoring and proof on every
path, aligned-direction constraints, repetitive or contrived alternatives, and
pressure toward burst-like presentation.

### B — every dual tag mixes same-cut and cross-cut-only histories

`U^{all-same-chi}_v` is empty. C151-A supplies one same-cut-positive history
for every dual tag, and each tag also has a distinct legal history on which
every joint opportunity is cross-cut.

B's strongest case is route-sensitive adjudication grammar. One route aligns
the decisive cuts; another separates them without adding a second player
decision. The positive path retains A's **direct** fit. On the negative path,
each form is **direct** at its own `chi`; cross-cut participation and the fixed-
tag contrast across histories are **partial**; convergence exclusion is
**neutral/protective**; and universal mixing is **aggregate**. Every negative-
path joint opportunity has an earlier-completing counterfactual, creating
speed/dominance pressure without guaranteeing visible delay or strategic
superiority. Costs are at least two histories and two adjudication grammars per
dual tag, exhaustive negative proof, timing opacity, and two-sided token-route
risk.

### C — saturated and path-mixed dual tags coexist

`U^{all-same-chi}_v` is a nonempty proper subset of `D^{dual}_v`. At least one
dual tag has a same-cut opportunity on every history. Another has C151-A's
positive history and a distinct cross-cut-only history.

C preserves universal same-cut semantic support for some Soul Relic results and
route-sensitive adjudication grammar for others, without yet guaranteeing a
player-visible difference. Local histories inherit A/B's ratings;
class coexistence is only **aggregate**. Costs are another subtype, likely
clarity or tempo premiums, disclosure/testing burden, saturation proof, and
negative/token-route proof.
