## Selected direction

The completed catalog must contain at least one reachable, materially
functional, bearer-compliant pure-action entry and one such pure-build entry,

## Sole active choice

C3c.32 applies only to counted positions in basis-sensitive entries, domain
`P+`. It classifies the minimum semantic facts needed, not record timestamps or
metadata. A delayed copy/hash/wrapper of frozen configuration remains
configuration-sufficient.

A configuration-sufficient position needs no semantic fact contingent on legal
post-entry combat history. An in-combat-required position needs a material fact
causally produced or changed by a contingent legal post-entry event or state
transition, proven through two reachable histories with identical frozen
configuration and deterministic initialization.

- **A — configuration-sufficient only:** every `P+` position can be supplied
  from frozen configuration and unavoidable initialization. This is the
  simplest loadout-planning model but is structurally close to a passive set
  bonus.
- **B, recommended — in-combat evidence required only:** every `P+` position
  requires a contingent combat-produced fact; configuration alone or its
  delayed serialization never suffices. This makes weaving combat-history-
  dependent but raises coordination, state, replay, and UI cost.
- **C — both evidence modes materially supported:** the catalog has material
  configuration-sufficient and in-combat-required witnesses. Later choices map
  modes to pure action/build entries and decide whether they share an entry or
  positions. This offers both static and live synergies but creates two player
  mental models and the largest validation surface.

The options exhaust configuration-sufficient/in-combat-required support within
`P+`. Positions in any later-admitted basis-neutral entry remain separate.
Event-versus-state subtype, declaration/resolution/outcome phase, mapping to
pure action/build entries, same-entry/position composition, inactive/dormant
visibility, source families and atoms, arity, operators, windows, reuse, Team
Tactic/environment admission, payoff, compatibility, horizons, persistence,
validation, and release remain later. An owner-written replacement may propose
a bounded third evidence horizon such as prior-encounter memory; A/B/C do not
authorize it.
