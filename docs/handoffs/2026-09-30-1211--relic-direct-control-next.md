card. They also found that the old short label “direct actual-result control”
could admit fake witnesses. The repaired semantic test is:

- at one held-fixed genuine conflict, two lawful participant-control vectors
  differ only in a current-result coordinate naming distinct admissible
  targets;
- all other participant and noncontrol coordinates remain identical; and
- the complete collision laws commit the respectively named targets while
  valid.

A target button or one-shot action whose only payload names the current target
is direct. A reusable priority, filter, weight, vote, conditional rule, or
resolver choice remains policy-mediated even when deterministic in the current
case. A compound target-plus-policy action is not independently direct unless
target varies with policy fixed. Confirmation, mandatory default, UI aliases,
upstream approval, and a different random realization do not create direct
control. Exact partial menus remain AUTHOR/SPEC. This is a wording repair, not
a new product choice or register row.

## Highest-value next step

Ask for B1 A, B, C, or an explicit replacement:

- **A — policy-mediated configurable contracts only. Qualified low-
  interruption/policy-mastery fallback.** No configurable contract directly
  names its current result. Every configurable controlled projection is
  `{P}`, with later `I` incidence possible. A removes direct result selection,
  but A7 still decides whether policy binds live or earlier.
- **B — every configurable contract is direct-capable somewhere. Agency/
  clarity escalation.** Every configurable fixed contract has at least one
  live result-naming opportunity. This does not mean direct-only or control at
  every opportunity; B2 still decides policy support.
- **C — direct-capable and policy-only configurable contracts coexist.
  Recommended.** At least one configurable contract supports direct result
  choice and at least one never does. With A3A-C this yields intrinsic,
  policy-only, and direct-capable Relic temperaments.

Illustratively, a Soul-answered Ashen may sometimes ask which valid `MERCY` or
`DEFIANCE` result manifests. An Oathbound Dreamglass may be configurable only
through a material resolution policy. An intrinsic Stone Witness follows its
own covenant. Names and dialect allocation remain later catalog/A3X work.

Recommend **C with A as the qualified low-interruption/policy-mastery
fallback**. C preserves dramatic live agency and prospective policy mastery
without making either universal. Its chief danger is a hidden tier ladder:
direct-capable Relics must not become the premium class while policy-only and
intrinsic Relics become consolation choices. Equal optimized Legendary
ceilings remain RCS-05/RCS-06/RCS-08 proof, not an excuse for unconditional
direct-choice power.

Fire A if live result choice repeatedly causes prompt fatigue, target shopping,
team quarterbacking, latency/default harm, or unavoidable option-value premium
while policy contracts remain legible, strategically rich, Rite-safe, and
capable of equal optimized ceilings. Escalate to B only if policy-only
settlement remains broadly opaque or unfair despite the best viable preview
and disclosure while direct choice remains fluid, non-token, non-dominant, and
thematically earned on every configurable contract.
