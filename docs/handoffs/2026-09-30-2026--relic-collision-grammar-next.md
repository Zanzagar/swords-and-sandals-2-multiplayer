## A6 adjudicated semantic boundary

The old “catalog-wide resolver versus definition-local kernels” wording was
vacuous if read as code structure. Any family of complete functions `κ_w` can
be wrapped in one dispatcher `K(w,o)`, and a universal interpreter can hide
arbitrary law programs. Three named-claim audits disagreed; a neutral
adjudicator kept A6 only as a player-facing transfer promise.

An admissible `Γ` is finite, versioned, and bounded. It has typed semantic
inputs, law-invariant operator meanings, fixed evaluation/composition order,
truthful normalization and certain/draw semantics, and counterfactual
invariants that players can use for forecast and counterplay. A law may bind
parameters or omit operators. It may not call `CUSTOM`, interpret arbitrary
programs or opaque tables, dispatch on law/version identity, or give nominal
modes unrelated law-specific meanings.

One function, several modules, shared helpers, copied code, tables, callbacks,
interpreters, and storage placement are implementation and prove neither
option.
