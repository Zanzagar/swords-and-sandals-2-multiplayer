## Selected direction

`T_D` is empty. Every final effective `p` in `P+` is exactly one of:

- `T_H`: it has a valid occurrence-history isolation witness and complete proof
  that no independently isolatable current-state path exists; or
- `T_S`: it has a valid current-state isolation witness and complete proof that
  no independently isolatable occurrence-history path exists.

C3c.34-C requires both `T_H` and `T_S` to be nonempty through distinct positions
in reachable, materially functional, bearer-compliant final entries. Incomplete
analysis satisfies neither type. The classification ignores additional coupled
boundaries still left open. It does not decide whether the two types share an
entry or Relic, their action/build or bearer/ally mapping, source atoms, reuse,
or double-count rules. This is a direction, not a complete accepted EP-D02
replacement, and changes no authoritative wording.

## Sole active choice

For each final effective `G` in `E+`, let `P(G)` be its nonempty counted
position set. An entry is:

- `E_H` when every position in `P(G)` is `T_H`;
- `E_S` when every position in `P(G)` is `T_S`; or
- `E_X` when `P(G)` contains at least one `T_H` and one `T_S` position.

These are independent-form classifications: extra coupled boundaries are
ignored, and unresolved position analysis passes no option. A cross-form entry
has distinct positions of each type but selects no AND/OR/order/simultaneity
rule and grants no permission for one source atom or event to fill both.

- **A — independent-form homogeneous entries only:** `E_X` is empty. Every
  entry uses one evidence clock. C3c.34-C forces at least one materially
  functional history-homogeneous entry and one distinct state-homogeneous entry.
  This is the clearest recipe language but forbids “past event plus live
  condition” recipes.
- **B — independent-form cross entries only:** `E_X=E+`. Every basis-sensitive
  entry contains distinct history-only and state-only positions. This forces
  both forms into every already-required pure-action and pure-build entry. It
  maximizes temporal integration but imposes history/state UI and regression
  burden on every recipe.
- **C, recommended — mixed homogeneous and cross-form portfolio:** `E_X` is a
  nonempty proper subset of `E+`. Require one materially functional cross-form
  entry and one distinct homogeneous entry. This preserves approachable one-
  clock recipes while permitting selected advanced “what happened plus what is
  true now” weaves. The cost is two entry teaching/validation standards and the
  risk that cross-form entries become brittle or dominate through compensating
  payoff.

Under C, the cross-form witness itself supplies both global evidence forms, so
the next dependent choice must decide whether the homogeneous complement
contains only `E_H`, only `E_S`, or both. Only afterward should entry types map
to action/build classes or stable Relic/resonance/Charm identities. A/B/C
exhaust zero, all, or a nonempty proper subset of `E+` being cross-form.

The at-least-two-source purpose and bearer-origin minimum apply to every entry.
All-bearer and bearer-plus-ally functional witnesses remain catalog-wide rather
than repeating for every temporal entry type. Exact subtypes, evaluation phase
or window, relationship operators, arity/roles, source families/atoms,
position-distinctness/reuse, origin mapping, basis-neutral admission, additional
coupled-boundary permission, payoff, UI, compatibility, horizons, persistence,
validation, and release remain open.
