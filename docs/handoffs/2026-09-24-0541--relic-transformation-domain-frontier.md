## Active `RCS-03A` card

Selected premises already require state-supporting Relics to occupy materially
different lineage-carried state assignments in one same-artifact history.
Therefore “state never changes” is not a legal branch. The genuine remaining
axis is whether a boundary-supporting Relic can own a mutable, remembered
evaluation-boundary attunement or merely express boundary forms supplied by
context.

The three exhaustive choices are:

- **A — remembered condition only:** persistent material transformation is
  confined to lineage-carried Relic state. Boundary variation remains
  contextual.
- **B — exceptional boundary-attuned Relics:** a nonempty proper subset of
  boundary-supporting definitions may remember and transform attunement; other
  boundary Relics remain contextual.
- **C — complete two-dialect transformation (recommended):** state-supporting
  Relics transform remembered condition, while every boundary-supporting Relic
  may transform remembered mode of listening. Definition purity remains: no
  name gets both clean transformation axes.

C best realizes the already-selected two-dialect catalog and every-artifact
biography without creating a hidden premium subclass. Its real cost is a second
transformation language plus boundary-shopping, snapshot-arbitrage, save, UI,
simulation, and balance pressure. Later binding, information, and lock cards
must control those risks.
