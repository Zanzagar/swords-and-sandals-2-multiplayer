### A — no strictly cross-cut joint opportunities — recommended

`K^{cross-op}_v` is empty. Every live joint opportunity on every legal history
of every dual tag satisfies `J^{same-chi}`: some distinct predicate- and
recurrence-isolating pair combination shares an exact anchored cut. Other pair
combinations at that opportunity may still use unequal cuts.

A provides one dependable aligned grammar and the strongest fixed-boundary
standing-ideal fit. Continuing context and both independent margins coexist
**directly** at every joint opportunity; unequal pair combinations and
downstream effects remain **partial**; excluding opportunities with no shared
cut is **neutral/protective**; universal opportunity/path/tag saturation is
**aggregate**. Costs are exhaustive synchronization and proof, constrained
authoring, repetitive or contrived foils, and pressure toward burst-like
adjudication. One obscure aligned combination per opportunity can still
satisfy A, so it does not guarantee a visible or valuable beat.

### B — every dual tag supports both grammars

`K^{cross-op}_v=D^{dual}_v`. Every dual tag has at least one strict cross-cut
joint opportunity. C152-A guarantees a distinct same-cut joint opportunity on
the same witness history.

B's strongest case is convergent and staged expression inside every dual Soul
Relic result. At a same-cut opportunity the fit is **direct**. At a cross-cut
opportunity each isolation is **direct** at its own `chi`, their relationship
across cuts is **partial**, and per-tag coexistence is **aggregate**. Costs are
two joint opportunities on a witness history per dual tag, exhaustive
negative proof, timing opacity, speed/dominance pressure, and token staging
unless a later materiality rule prevents it.

### C — fully same-cut and cross-cut-capable tags coexist

`K^{cross-op}_v` is a nonempty proper subset of `D^{dual}_v`. At least one dual
tag has no strict cross-cut joint opportunity anywhere, while another has at
least one.

C creates synchronized and timing-variable Soul Relic archetypes. The no-
cross class inherits A's **direct** local fit; the cross-capable class inherits
B's direct/partial ratings; class coexistence is only **aggregate**. Costs are
another subtype, likely clarity or tempo premiums, universal proof for one
class, witness proof for the other, and balance pressure between timing
grammars.
