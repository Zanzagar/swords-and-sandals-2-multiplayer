## Sole active choice

C3c.7-A makes Relics discrete unique instances. C3c.11-A permits at most one
owned instance of a definition per combatant and created no rolls or evolution.
Before deciding which identity owns a base recipe or which identities cover the
six cells, C3c.39 asks whether same-definition Relic instances may have
materially different final input predicates **within one fixed temporal-by-
basis cell**.

For a valid variability witness, compare two actor-ID-preserving counterfactual
legal worlds for the same combatant. Each world contains exactly one of two
same-definition instances; the instances never coexist. Hold the combatant,
ruleset/catalog version, Bound Soul, input-Charm mechanical descriptor,
qualifying source records, all other semantic facts, and the cell fixed. Only a
reachable predicate-result difference caused by instance-specific Relic data
counts. A different ID, provenance label, history, or cosmetic alone does not;
a difference realized only by changing cells does not.

- **A, recommended — definition-stable within-cell input grammar:** no
  supported definition has such instance variation. Complete analysis must show
  invariance under every legal same-definition substitution within each cell in
  which that definition participates. A named Relic remains predictable while
  resonance and input Charms provide the explicit variation layers. Instance
  IDs retain ownership, custody, and provenance value. The cost is no rolled or
  evolved relationship variants inside one recipe class.
- **B — every definition is within-cell instance-variable:** every supported
  definition needs a material fixed-cell counterfactual witness. This makes each
  copy a singular artifact, but adds a universal third grammar-variation layer,
  complicates explanation/testing/migration, and is acquisition-hostile while
  duplicate awards redirect instead of supplying a comparison copy.
- **C — mixed stable and within-cell-variable definitions:** at least one
  materially participating definition must pass the complete invariance rule,
  and at least one other needs B's material witness. This reserves unique-copy
  behavior for special artifact fantasies but creates two collection and
  comparison languages.

A/B/C exhaust empty, universal, and nonempty-proper instance-variable subsets
of supported definitions. They do not decide whether instance identity may
change temporal/basis cell; that remains a separate later choice under every
option. They also do not decide payoff variability, input-Charm instance
granularity, base-grammar ownership, entry multiplicity, transformation powers
or precedence, cell mapping, operators, atoms, arity, origins, coupling,
payoffs, compatibility, horizons, persistence, tuning, validation, or release.
