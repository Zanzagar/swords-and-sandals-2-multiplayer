## Semantic boundary

Inside the nonempty exact-`{P}` class, policy is independently variable only
when two lawful settings on one matched reachable conflict:

- keep every other participant-semantic value completely fixed;
- change the collision-policy value; and
- change the complete target law or distribution.

This is semantic local separability. Two widgets with forced diagonal pairs
remain bundled. One opaque whole-stance selector may still be independent. A
standalone policy qualifies because its remaining semantic projection is
empty. One held-fixed fiber suffices; a full Cartesian product does not.
