## C137 anti-laundering correction

Adversarial review found that the first generic “whether/when/reachable” phrase
could let concede versus continue or a long unrelated action versus a short
one qualify. That contradicted the card's express rule that unrelated decisions
do not count.

The corrected material-opportunity test requires a reachable isolation pair to
change, before terminal resolution, at least one of:

1. an `H` or `S` fact materially read by the fixed relationship;
2. the fixed context's operative availability; or
3. tag-specific recurrence authorization.

That semantic difference must be capable of changing whether, or at which
later semantic cut, renewed sufficiency plus recurrence authorization occur.
Elapsed time, action duration, generic phase advancement, encounter
termination, or globally making every result unreachable does not count by
itself. This correction implements the selected meaning; it chooses no actor,
source, duration, or chosen rebuild action.

## Why context availability is next

expressly permits a temporarily unavailable context to return as the same tag.
C137-A requires a material choice while the relationship is insufficient, but

choice could therefore power-cycle the context and rearm a relationship that
was never confronted as false inside its live evaluator.

Retained-strand or source-continuity questions are premature until this
context-level distinction is settled. A strand is not well formed at a cut
where its containing context cannot evaluate it, and canonical source atoms,
provenance, operators, and reuse are independently unresolved.
