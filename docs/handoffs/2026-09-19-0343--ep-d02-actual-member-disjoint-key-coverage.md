## Exact graph boundary and witness

The overlap and member-disjoint predicates are mutually exclusive. Covering
three vertices with overlap edges needs at least two edges; covering them with
member-disjoint edges needs two different edges. Only three pairs exist, so
three keys cannot have both kinds of coverage. Four keys suffice.

```text

b0  FS->abc  VW->abd  TR->cde   M0={abc,abd,cde}
b1  VT->abc  FW->cde  SR->abe   M1={abc,abe,cde}

b3  FS->abd  VT->acd  WR->bce   M3={abd,acd,bce}
```

retains `acd` on T and is not a common edge, proving no every-pair rule.
