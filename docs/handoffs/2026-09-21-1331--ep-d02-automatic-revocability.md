`V^{auto}_v` is empty. Once a positive receipt candidate is provisionally

### B — every tagged candidate is materially revocable — strongest countercase

`V^{auto}_v=K^{candidate}_v`. Every context/receipt tag has a deterministic
preserve-versus-revoke witness. This means universal susceptibility, not
inevitable failure: every tag retains a committing arm and its required
reachable occurrence. It gives commit-time truth and lets meaningful automatic
expiry reject stale or transient setups, but creates no-agency fizzles, timing
opacity, replay/order burden, and fragile current-state recipes. Each real
preserve/revoke relationship is **partial** across that automatic-evolution
boundary; universal prevalence is **aggregate**.
