## Active owner choice — EP-D02-C3c.99

Let `A^{adm}_v(d)` contain the hypothetical noncore Soul-map assignments
allowed by locked definition `d`'s already-selected stable palette/core,
singleton-map size and count, full noncore union, block-size profile, and
dialect constraints. For assignment `x`, let `M(x)` be its complete unordered
map family.

Definition `d` is support-positive exactly when some `x,y in A^{adm}_v(d)`
satisfy:

```text
M(x) != M(y)
M(x) intersect M(y) is nonempty
x(s) != y(s) for every supported Soul s
```

Thus an exact noncore map survives as a family member but must move to a
disjoint Soul block, because no Soul keeps its own map. This is legal capacity,
not an authored operative key.


### A — no locked definition supports shared-member common pairs

Every legal common edge necessarily uses member-disjoint families. A creates
the clearest assignment-robust whole-roster foil and retains the compact
degree-five geometry. Its cost is forbidding an exact relational motif from
continuing while every Soul's relation changes. Shared atomic labels and the
stable full union still persist. Tight floors are `6/9/54`; tight maximum
degree is five.

### B — support-positive and support-negative definitions coexist

At least one locked name can support shared-member reassignment and another is
limited to member-disjoint common edges. B preserves a compact teaching lane
beside an advanced relational lane, but adds a hidden geometry subtype, token
support risk, and a second locked definition. Tight floors are `7/11/66`;
tight maximum degree is six.

### C — every locked definition supports it — recommended

Every locked name's stable geometry admits some unequal-family common pair with
an exact family member preserved and reassigned. C gives the whole class room
for the strongest current **Souls and Simulacra** pair grammar and avoids a
support-only subtype. It does not author the pair. Costs are universal degree-
six geometry at the six-Soul floor, a larger family/assignment proof surface,
exclusion of compact degree-five Veil, and latent-content risk. Tight floors
remain `6/9/54`; tight maximum degree is six.

A/B/C are exhaustive because the support-positive definition class is empty,
nonempty proper, or whole. All preserve Soul counts
`{6,8,9,10,12,14,15}`. B needs one two-key locked definition of each support
sign; support itself adds no operative key.

At one fixed exact-key/Soul-roster relationship, inherited fit remains
**direct**. Support is **neutral**, and its prevalence is **aggregate**. A
later realized shared-member common pair would be **partial** at both the Soul
and family boundaries: definition, core, union, and an exact map continue while
every Soul's assigned map differs across separate simulacra. C is recommended
for coherent future capacity, not because latent legality itself satisfies the
standing ideal. A's assignment-robust simplicity is the strongest countercase.
