## Selected direction

Let nonempty `I` contain every materially functional input-Charm definition,
and let `I_E` contain those definitions with at least one material active use
in an `E+` final entry. C3c.40-B sets `I_E=I`.

Every definition in `I` therefore needs at least one legal instance and one
reachable, bearer-compliant entry in at least one of C3c.38-B's six temporal-
by-basis cells where the active exact-instance world and an otherwise-identical
legal empty-input-lane world make the final predicate return different results.
An authored label, equipped but inert Charm, unreachable entry, or
extensionally identical predicate rewrite does not count.

This is existential per definition. It does not require every instance, every
use, or every one of the six cells to participate; it does not require a
distinct Charm definition per cell or select a stable identity-to-cell map. A
participating definition may also support basis-neutral `E0`, but C3c.40-B
neither requires nor forbids any such use or content. Exact cells, instance
sensitivity, cross-cell behavior, grammar/content, transformation precedence,
and payoff-side rules remain open. C3c.40-B is a direction rather than an
accepted EP-D02 replacement and changes no authoritative wording.

The first illustrative C3c.40-B example mixed action names with supplying-

replaced with a clean pure-action history example. The owner selected

## Sole active choice

C3c.8-A gives Charms unique instance IDs, and C3c.40-B guarantees a nonempty
`E+` participant domain. C3c.41 asks whether same-definition input-Charm
instances can produce different final predicate results while the exact Soul
Relic instance/state, Bound Soul, exact payoff-Charm instance/state or absence,
canonical source/history/state valuation, evaluation boundary, version, every
other semantic fact, and one `E+` temporal-by-basis cell remain fixed.

Let `V_I` contain definitions having at least one reachable material witness
between two distinct same-definition/version instances in actor-ID-preserving
alternate legal worlds, each using exactly one active input Charm. Both final
predicates must remain in the same cell and return different Booleans on one
identical source/history/state valuation reachable in both worlds. The compared
instances need not coexist in that combatant's collection or history and are
not active together for that combatant in either witness world; this creates no
rule for other combatants. Different IDs, provenance, serialization, labels,
or cosmetics without a predicate-result difference do not count; payoff-only,
legality-only, unreachable, and cross-cell differences do not count.

- **A, recommended — every definition is stable within `E+`:** `V_I` is
  empty. Every definition passes complete same-definition invariance in every
  `E+` cell in which it participates. Mixed Relic instances retain the
  exceptional personal-artifact role, while named input Charms remain
  dependable within a fixed root, context, and cell. The cost is no per-copy
  variation inside that fixed cell; cross-cell behavior remains open.
- **B — every definition varies within `E+`:** `V_I=I`. Every definition needs
  at least one fixed-Relic, fixed-cell material instance witness. This makes
  every Charm acquisition potentially singular but compounds Relic, Charm,
  and resonance opacity and creates later disclosure, comparison, migration,
  and bad-outcome-safeguard questions for every definition.
- **C — mixed stable and variable definitions:** `V_I` is a nonempty proper
  subset of `I`, deriving at least two definitions. At least one definition
  passes A's invariance rule and one distinct definition has B's witness. This
  supports exceptional faceted Charms but creates a second mixed taxonomy on
  top of mixed Relics.

A/B/C exhaust empty/all/nonempty-proper `V_I`. Incomplete reachable-domain
analysis proves neither stability nor variation and remains unresolved. B/C
require mechanically distinct same-definition instances in alternate legal
worlds evaluated on one identical valuation reachable in both for every or
selected definitions, but require no same-combatant co-possession and select no
duplicate-award path, instance-state realization mechanism, disclosure,
choice, reroll, re-attunement, value, sidegrade safeguard, or bad-outcome
protection. A may lack any nontrivial comparison when fewer than two distinct
same-definition instances are legally comparable in a common fixed-
root/context/cell domain and promises no interchangeable copies. B/C do not
require both compared variants to differ from the empty lane or be viable
sidegrades. Behavior in `E0`, cross-cell variation, same-instance evolution,
payoff-Charm instance sensitivity, mapping, ownership of base grammar, exact
content, acquisition,
horizons, persistence, validation, and release remain open.
