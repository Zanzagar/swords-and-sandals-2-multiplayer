## Sole active choice

- **A, recommended — every exact realization keeps one coarse anchor class
  across Souls:** `V^Q_{S,v}` is empty. Every exact `b` is uniformly multi-
  anchor or uniformly one-anchor across all supported Souls. C3c.56-B forces
  at least one exact realization of each class. This creates a stable coarse
  one-/multi-anchor class for later teaching while leaving exact singleton
  labels and multi-anchor counts open. The cost is that Soul cannot move fixed `b` across
  the threshold, and every uniformly one-anchor Soul fiber repeats its
  coupled-only burden.
- **B — mix class-stable and class-variable exact realizations:**
  `V^Q_{S,v}` is a nonempty proper subset. At least one `b` crosses the
  threshold for some Soul pair, while another remains uniformly multi-anchor
  or uniformly one-anchor. The stable fiber's polarity is unselected. This
  permits selected Soul-sensitive causal texture but creates an exact-
  instance/state/boundary taxonomy that may be invisible, and one obscure
  second singleton can satisfy the variable witness.
- **C — every exact realization changes coarse anchor class across Souls:**
  `V^Q_{S,v}=R_v*`. Every exact `b` has at least one multi-anchor Soul and one
  one-anchor Soul while its total cell palette stays fixed. This maximizes
  Soul-sensitive causal structure but imposes the universal positive/negative
  matrix. One Soul could receive one obscure second singleton across every
  `b`, producing a repeated Soul-indexed multi-anchor/one-anchor split without
  proving visibility, fun, balance, ease, or power.
