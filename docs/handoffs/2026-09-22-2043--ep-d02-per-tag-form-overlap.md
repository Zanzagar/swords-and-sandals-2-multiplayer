### A — every tag supports both forms

`D^{dual}_v=K^{candidate}_v`. Every tag has at least one `I_P` and one `I_R`
witness. A does not require both on every path, in one encounter, or at one
opportunity.

This is the strongest flexibility countercase. No authored tag lacks either
reset vocabulary. Each actual isolation is **direct** at `chi`; same-tag
continuity across different witnesses is **partial**; per-path co-location is
**neutral**; universal dual-tag prevalence is **aggregate**. Costs are
universal double authoring and validation, weaker tag identity, homogeneous-
menu pressure, and a token loophole: one hidden second-form witness per tag
satisfies A.

### B — every tag specializes in exactly one form

`D^{dual}_v` is empty. Every tag is predicate-only or recurrence-only. C142-C
therefore requires at least one tag of each specialist class. C141-A makes each
tag's sole form occur on every legal path.

This is the strongest identity and anti-token countercase. Each tag teaches one
stable reset grammar while the catalog keeps both relationship-rebuilding and
invocation play. Each actual `chi` is **direct**; the recurrence is **partial**;
excluding within-tag duality is **neutral/protective**; cross-tag coexistence is
**aggregate**. Costs are exhaustive negative proof for every tag, categorical
silos, no same-tag expression of both disciplines, and the remaining risk that
one whole specialist class exists only on a rare tag.

### C — dual-support and specialist tags coexist — recommended

`D^{dual}_v` is a nonempty proper subset. At least one tag supports both forms
and at least one supports exactly one. C does not require both predicate-only
and recurrence-only specialist classes; the dual tag can supply both global
forms.

C keeps clear teaching identities while allowing selected relationships to
express the richer Souls and Simulacra fantasy of both bond rebuilding and
manifestation authorization. Each `chi` is **direct**; same-tag dual expression
across distinct histories is **partial**; specialist exclusion is
**neutral/protective**; catalog coexistence is **aggregate**. Costs are a new
taxonomy, disclosure/testing burden, likely flexibility-premium pressure, and
a token-dual loophole until later path breadth and non-dominance rules.
