## Active frontier

- **A — no equal-size definition varies its exact partition:** every name keeps
  fixed cohorts. This is neutral/protective at C79 and retains direct fixed-key
  fit. It is easiest to teach and avoids preferred-partner realization risk,
  but leaves the equal-size class's partner-rewiring capacity unused.
- **B — stable and variable equal-size definitions coexist:** one name keeps
  fixed cohorts and another realizes a peer reshuffle. Variable-name cross-key
  fit is partial and coexistence aggregate. It offers beginner/advanced
  texture, but immediately splits C78-A's equal-size dialect into a fourth
  subtype and requires another definition and comparison marker.
- **C, recommended — every equal-size definition varies its exact partition:**
  every name realizes at least two partner arrangements while each Soul keeps
  its common size role. This completes C78-A's clean three-dialect language and
  turns latent relational capability into real content without B's extra
  subtype. Cross-key fit is partial, not direct, because the arrangements live
  in separate exact simulacra. The costs are universal comparison/UI work and
  hidden-roll or premium-partner risk unless later disclosure, owner-Soul
  relevance, agency, and sidegrade rules constrain it.
