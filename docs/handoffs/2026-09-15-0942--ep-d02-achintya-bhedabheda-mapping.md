## Thematic mapping

Credible scholarly and tradition sources agree on the important qualifier:
*acintya-bhedābheda* concerns unfathomable **simultaneous** oneness and
difference, especially the relation between Krishna and his energies or living
selves. It does not mean unity in one state and difference in another.

The direct mechanical analogy is therefore already inside a fixed exact Relic
map:

- C64-A makes every changed Soul-pair map both genuinely unequal and
  non-disjoint.
- A single-core affinity makes this most visible: its one universal anchor is
  continuity/non-difference, while its rotating Soul-specific remainder is
  real difference at the same time.
- A prism is a more relational dialect: every Soul map is distinct, while every
  pair retains overlap even though no one label is universal.

C69-C is only superficially the “both” answer. It requires every multi-support
definition to possess a prism exact key and an affinity exact key, but those are
separate keys; C69 explicitly authorizes no simultaneous coexistence, state
transition, or live switching. B has the same limitation for a subset of
definitions.

Consequently, if forced among C69's options, A remains the best thematic fit.
Each named Relic keeps one stable dialect of simultaneous continuity and
difference. B/C add metamorphic breadth rather than a stronger bhedābheda
relationship. This is a game-design analogy, not a claim of theological
equivalence.
