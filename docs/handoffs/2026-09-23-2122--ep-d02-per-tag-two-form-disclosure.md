### A — every dual tag exposes both forms — recommended

`D^{pre-both}_v=D^{dual}_v`. Every mechanically dual tag has at least one
qualifying pre-choice P account and one qualifying R account. The two witnesses
may use different histories, opportunities, pairs, authorized references, or
lawfully human-controllable command-holder roles.

Every dual Relic then gives players truthful causal vocabulary for both faces
of its bond-versus-manifestation mechanic. Each account is **direct** at its
own `O^-` boundary; the relationship between accounts distributed across
boundaries remains **partial**; excluding a hidden second form is
**neutral/protective**; and universal per-tag overlap is **aggregate**.

Costs are two explanation/localization/validation obligations per tag, greater
cognitive load, less causal mystery, homogeneous-teaching pressure, and token
compliance through one obscure second-form account. A does not make both
accounts share a history, opportunity, explanation, command-holder, or player.

Example: Ashen and Dreamglass each expose both P and R accounts, though each
tag's accounts may live on different legal histories.

### B — every dual tag exposes exactly one form

`D^{pre-both}_v` is empty. Because C157-A excludes empty support, every tag is
exactly P-only or R-only. Because C156-A keeps both forms in the catalog, both
singleton classes must be nonempty. There are no opaque tags.

This is the strongest stable teaching-identity countercase: each Relic teaches
one causal language cleanly while leaving its other mechanically real face
experiential or retrospective. The supported form is **direct**; the absent
form is **neutral/unsupported** at the pre-choice information boundary;
teaching focus can be **neutral/protective**; and cross-tag P/R coexistence is
**aggregate**.

Costs are prospective access to only half of every dual Relic's causal grammar,
guide dependence, and risk that the hidden form reads as secondary or
inconsistent.

Example: Ashen is P-only after exhaustive exclusion of R; Dreamglass is R-only
after exhaustive exclusion of P. Both remain mechanically P/R-dual.

### C — both-form and singleton-supported tags coexist

`D^{pre-both}_v` is a nonempty proper subset of `D^{dual}_v`. At least one tag
exposes both forms and at least one other exposes exactly one. C does not
require both singleton polarities: the complement may be only P-only, only
R-only, or contain both.

This permits fully articulated covenants beside deliberately focused
simulacra. Both-form members inherit A's ratings, singleton members inherit
B's, and class coexistence is **aggregate**. Costs are a disclosure taxonomy,
unequal learning burden, likely clarity/flexibility premiums, and token both-
form membership.

Example: Ashen exposes P and R; Dreamglass exposes P only. An R-only tag is
permitted but not required.

## Proof, floors, anti-laundering, and deferrals
