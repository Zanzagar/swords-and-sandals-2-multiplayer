## Design answer

Multiple **owned** Relics create collection breadth without competing on the
active build surface. One active root gives the Relic layer one focal identity,
one Bound-Soul resonance pair, and one weave graph; later Charm rules can add
depth. Two active roots can be presented inside one visually grouped region,
but they remain two mechanical graphs and require later source-reuse and payoff-
stacking rules. Presentation can contain that complexity but cannot remove it.

Recommendation remains **A — maximum one active root**. B remains defensible if
the owner values dual-Relic combinations enough to accept the interaction
surface. C creates the greatest thematic and mechanical competition.
