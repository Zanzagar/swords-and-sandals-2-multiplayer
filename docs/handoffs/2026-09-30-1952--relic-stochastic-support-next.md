## A5 semantic boundary

Retain A4's nonempty set `W_v^auto` of canonical complete automatic laws. For
each law, fix one reachable automatic occurrence's contract/version, bound
whole covenant and other participant settings, immutable Relic completion,
source revision/cut, distinct proposed-target set, complete contributor

committed authoritative state. The law is stochastic-capable exactly when at
least one such occurrence gives at least two distinct canonical proposed

current draw a callback earlier is not. A declared seeded generator may
