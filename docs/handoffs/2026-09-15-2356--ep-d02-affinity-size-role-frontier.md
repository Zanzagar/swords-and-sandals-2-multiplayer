## Active frontier

- **A — every mixed-size affinity definition is size-role-stable:**
  `D^mu_{var,v}` is empty. A named Soul keeps one group size under a given
  mixed name. This is the safest learnability rule and prevents preferred
  majority/minority realizations, but can freeze hidden Soul castes; with
  profile `(4,2)`, a stable role map fixes the whole partition. Fixed-key fit
  remains direct; A's marginal cross-key contribution is neutral/protective.
- **B, recommended for gameplay — stable and variable mixed definitions
  coexist:** `D^mu_{var,v}` is nonempty proper. Stable mixed names teach the
  baseline while exceptional “morphic” names recast relational breadth across
  simulacra. This fits **Souls and Simulacra**, but its cross-key ideal fit is
  only partial and the coexistence across names aggregate. B forces at least
  two mixed definitions and is recommended only if later rules require visible
  cause and role preview, owner-Soul relevance, player-actionable commitment,
  and qualitative sidegrades rather than hidden premium realizations.
- **C — every mixed-size affinity definition is size-role-variable:**
  `D^mu_{var,v}=D^mixed_v`. Every mixed name gets alternate size-role maps;
  equal-size names remain forced stable under C76-B. This maximizes morphic
  support but universalizes paired authoring, comparison, migration, and
  preferred-realization pressure. Per-name cross-key fit is at most partial;
  the universal catalog mandate is aggregate, not a stronger direct fit.
