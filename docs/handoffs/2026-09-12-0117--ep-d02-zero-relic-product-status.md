## Why the card changed

The owner correctly asked how zero Relics could be power-equivalent when the
point of Relics is meaningful output. The answer is that legal omission alone
does not provide equivalence. Supporting equivalence requires a mutually
exclusive alternative archetype; otherwise an optimized mature build will
normally want the additional Relic option value. The first C3c.9 alternatives
and recommendation were withdrawn before selection.

## Sole active choice

- **A — legal omission without a parity promise:** zero remains legal and has
  no dedicated substitute, fallback, or competitive guarantee;
- **B — competitively supported no-Relic archetype:** zero remains legal, and
  the complete design supplies a mutually exclusive alternative opportunity
  intended for a comparable eventual performance envelope; exact content and
  budget remain later;
- **C, recommended — mandatory after safe late enablement:** zero is legal
  before a later readiness transition, but afterward every combat in that
  enabled ruleset requires an active personal Relic and the combatant cannot
  opt back out.

C most honestly makes the Relic weaver a core late-game layer without creating
B's shadow progression system. Its cost is a permanent post-transition
cognitive/inventory obligation and no mature Relicless archetype. The enabled
transition is invalid unless later acquisition, loss, and migration rules
guarantee a legal personal instance plus a non-stranding recovery path. That is
a safety gate, not a selected starter Relic, milestone, rate, or patch.

Boundary: pre-enable Aster may enter with zero. Under A, mature Aster may also
enter but receives no substitute or parity promise. Under B, a mature zero-root
build must later receive a mutually exclusive supported opportunity. Under C,
mature Aster's zero-root proposal fails; if Aster has no legal Relic, enablement
must fail safely rather than strand the save. No option requires a Charm or
guarantees a currently satisfiable weave.
