The first internal post-C103 draft moved directly to actual member-disjoint-
edge coverage of every key. That was premature and was withdrawn before owner
presentation. C102-C proves only that each definition has some admissible
disjoint family pair. It does not prove that every family carried by a
completed key has any admissible disjoint partner. Actual coverage would
conflate structural incapability with deliberate nonuse.

Define

`H^R_v(d)={b in B_v(d):exists F in F^{adm}_v(d), F intersect M_v(b) empty}`

`D^{H-R}_{full,v}={d in D^{U=}_{locked,v}:H^R_v(d)=B_v(d)}`.

edge belong to `H^R`; it does not settle a third or later key. This support
card must precede actual incidence by the same factorization discipline used

### A — no locked definition has full per-key support

`D^{H-R}_{full,v}` is empty. Every locked name contains at least one completed
key whose family has no admissible disjoint partner, even while another pair

### B — full-support and support-incomplete definitions coexist

`D^{H-R}_{full,v}` is a nonempty proper subset. At least one name gives every
key a hypothetical disjoint partner while another contains a support-isolated
key.

B could reserve a visibly rooted simulacrum for selected names. It is the
strongest gameplay countercase if that identity later becomes disclosed and
valuable; without those rules it is a hidden geometry subtype. It works only
at ten or fifteen Souls and has tight floors `7/13/130` at ten Souls.

### C — every locked definition has full per-key support — recommended

`D^{H-R}_{full,v}=D^{U=}_{locked,v}`. Every completed exact key has at least one
admissible hypothetical family disjoint from its own, although that partner
need not be authored.

C preserves every retained Soul count, avoids a support-only subtype, and
makes the later actual key-coverage choice honest. Its costs are excluding
thirty otherwise-legal isolated five-family shapes from completed-key use,
per-key proof burden, and latent-content risk. Tight floors remain `6/10/60`.
