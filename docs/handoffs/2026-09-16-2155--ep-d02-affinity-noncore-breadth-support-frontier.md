## Frontier audit

C71-A gives definition `d` one total cell palette `C_v(d)` and degree
`d_v(d)`. C72-A gives it one per-Soul singleton count `k_v(d)`. C73-A gives
each affinity definition one distinct singleton-map count `rho_v(d)`. C80-A
gives it one sole all-Soul core `kappa_v(d)`. The ambient noncore palette

`C_v(d)\{kappa_v(d)}`

therefore already has stable size `N_v(d)=d_v(d)-1`; that cannot be the next
choice. For exact affinity key `b`, instead define

`U_v(b)=union_{s in S_v}(I_v(b,s)\{kappa_v(d)})`

and

`u_v(b)=|U_v(b)|`.

`U_v(b)` is the noncore label vocabulary receiving at least one singleton
grounding somewhere across the roster. An ambient label outside `U_v(b)` is
still in the total palette through coupled-only support; it is not absent or
dead. Breadth `u` is coarser than exact `U` identity, complete map-family
identity, or assignment of maps to Soul blocks.

Remove the core from each distinct Soul map. One exact key becomes a family of
`rho_v(d)` distinct `t_v(d)=k_v(d)-1` element subsets of an `N_v(d)`-label
palette. Their total intersection is empty; otherwise a second noncore label
would be an all-Soul core. Let `W_v(d)` collect the attainable union
cardinalities of all legal families with that fixed tuple, and define

`D^u_{support,v}={d in D_{affinity,v}: |W_v(d)|>=2}`.

This support atom must precede actual breadth variation. Many tuples force one
`u`; those definitions are ineligible, not authored “stable” witnesses for a
later realized-variation coexistence rule. Skipping support would repeat the
support/behavior bundling corrected before C75, C78, and C81.

With at most six causal labels, exhaustive enumeration gives support exactly
for stable tuples `(degree,k,rho)`:

- `(5,3,3)`;
- `(6,3,rho)` for `3<=rho<=6`; and
- `(6,4,4)`.

No other legal tuple supports two union cardinalities. In particular, `k=2`
forces `u=rho`, and `rho=2` plus empty intersection forces the two noncore maps
disjoint and `u=2(k-1)`.
