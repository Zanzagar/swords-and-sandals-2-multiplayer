## Selected direction

`E_X` is a nonempty proper subset of `E+`. The completed catalog must contain:

- at least one reachable, materially functional, bearer-compliant cross-form
  final entry containing distinct `T_H` and `T_S` positions; and
- at least one distinct reachable, materially functional, bearer-compliant
  homogeneous final entry whose positions are all `T_H` or all `T_S`.

Every position remains independently single-form under C3c.35-A. The cross-form
witness supplies both catalog-wide evidence forms, so C3c.36-C does not decide
which homogeneous subtype exists. It also selects no operator, evaluation
window, arity, source atom/reuse rule, action/build or bearer/ally mapping,
stable Relic/resonance/Charm mapping, or additional coupled-boundary policy.
This is a direction, not a complete accepted EP-D02 replacement, and changes no
authoritative wording.

## Sole active choice

Let `E_O = E+ \ E_X` be the selected nonempty homogeneous complement. Every
entry in it is independently history-homogeneous (`E_H`) or independently
state-homogeneous (`E_S`). Only reachable, materially functional, bearer-
compliant final entries count as support; an authored label, unreachable entry,
or unresolved position classification does not.

- **A — history-homogeneous support only:** `E_H` is nonempty and `E_S` is
  empty. Cross-form entries remain required. Every state-only position must
  therefore occur inside a cross-form entry. This provides approachable
  sequence-memory recipes but makes every live-state concept pay the two-clock
  complexity tax.
- **B — state-homogeneous support only:** `E_S` is nonempty and `E_H` is empty.
  Cross-form entries remain required. Every history-only position must occur
  inside a cross-form entry. This provides approachable live-condition recipes
  but makes every remembered-event concept pay the two-clock tax.
- **C, recommended — both homogeneous types materially supported:** require
  distinct functional `E_H` and `E_S` witnesses in addition to the inherited
  `E_X` witness. This derives at least three distinct final entries without
  requiring three Relic definitions. It provides focused occurrence-memory,
  focused live-state, and advanced cross-form recipes. The cost is three
  teaching/testing patterns and the risk of filler or a strict complexity/power
  ladder if later content does not give each a real niche.

A/B/C exhaust the nonempty support sets `{E_H}`, `{E_S}`, and `{E_H,E_S}`.
“Neither” contradicts C3c.36-C's required homogeneous entry. Any genuinely
third independent evidence form would explicitly reopen the upstream H/S
decisions; a content subtype inside H or S does not create another option.

After C3c.37, the next semantic dependency is mapping the surviving temporal
entry types across C3c.31-A's pure-action and pure-build classes. Stable
Relic/resonance/Charm identity mapping remains a separate later axis. The
at-least-two-source and bearer-origin rules apply to every entry; all-bearer and
bearer-plus-ally functional witnesses remain catalog-wide. Exact subtypes,
phase/window, operators/order, arity/roles, atoms/distinctness/reuse, origin
mapping, basis-neutral admission, extra coupling, payoff, UI, compatibility,
horizons, persistence, validation, and release remain open.
