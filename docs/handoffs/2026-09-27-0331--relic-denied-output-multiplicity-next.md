## Present exactly this next

Let `X^{deny}_v` contain reachable fixed denied-pair base opportunities after
the pair/proofs, false permission result, applicable contracts, upstream
routing stance, factual context, and every non-F input are fixed, but before
any F-owned setting, completion rule, or random realization. Let
`L^{deny}_v(x)` be the nonempty finite set of canonical lawful pair-local
output identity sets, and let `M^{deny}_v` contain the opportunities with at
least two such outputs.

- **A — every fixed opportunity is single-valued:** `M^{deny}_v` is empty.
  Once the complete base opportunity is known, exactly one output is lawful.
  Different disclosed contracts or contexts may still deterministically emit
  `{}`, `{S}`, `{S1,S2}`, or different exact substitutes. **Recommended.**
  E1-E4 already supply policy diversity; A prevents denial from becoming an
  output shop, improves causal explanation, team planning, replay trust, and

  beside routing. It is neutral/protective ideal fit and prunes F2A-F3.
- **B — some fixed opportunity retains several lawful outputs:**
  `M^{deny}_v` is nonempty. At least one held-fixed base opportunity has two or
  more canonical lawful dispositions. B opens a real resolution surface but
  selects no participant authority, policy language, system priority, or
  randomness. Its strongest case is a small prospectively committed
  orientation that makes the same relationship settle differently in a way
  clearer contracts or contexts cannot reproduce. Multiplicity itself is not
  agency or direct ideal fit. Later cards must defeat dominant “always take
  two,” favorable-resolution, quarterbacking, grief, retry, and stochastic-
  laundering failures.

A/B are exhaustive. Same-shape exact alternatives count only when their
canonical receipt identity sets are genuinely distinct. Payoff-only variants,
aliases, and outputs belonging to different fixed opportunities do not count.
Under B, universal-versus-mixed prevalence remains AUTHOR/SPEC unless a later
audit finds a separate player-material consequence.

Concrete boundary: under A, one fixed **Shattered Concord** opportunity has
exactly one lawful `{S1,S2}` result, although another disclosed context may
fuse or sever. Under B, one fixed **Prismatic Concord** base opportunity
retains both `{S}` and `{S1,S2}` before later resolution; the player still may
not choose after seeing the denial.
