## Frontier audit

Per-key overlap-edge coverage is the preferred next structural anti-token
descendant. It exactly mirrors C97-to-C98: C100 supplies at least one qualifying
edge per name, while C101 asks whether every authored vertex participates.
There is no missing structural prerequisite. Predicate/receipt continuity,
controlled matched substitution, comparable access, disclosure, agency, and
value are independent and more gameplay-material siblings, not facts derived
here.

For each locked definition `d`, let

`K^O_v(d)=union O^W_v(d)={b in B_v(d) : exists b'!=b with {b,b'} in O^W_v(d)}`

and

`D^{K-O}_{full,v}={d in D^{U=}_{locked,v} : K^O_v(d)=B_v(d)}`.

C100-C guarantees `O^W_v(d)` is nonempty in every name and therefore already
covers at least two keys. Option A must be described as **no definition is
fully covered**, never as no keys being covered. C98-C gives every key a common
all-Soul-changing partner, but that partner may use a family disjoint from the
key's family.

## Active owner choice — EP-D02-C3c.101

```text
ONE OVERLAP-ORPHAN KEY — degree 6, profile (2,2,2)
core h omitted; U={a,b,c,d,e}

b0  F,S->abc   V,W->abd   T,R->cde   M0={abc,abd,cde}

u   S,T->abe   F,V->acd   W,R->bcd   Mu={abe,acd,bcd}
```

`b0/b1` changes every Soul and shares `abc,cde`, so it is an overlap-bearing
common edge. `b0/u` changes every Soul but its families are member-disjoint.
`b1/u` shares `abe`, but S retains `abe`, so that pair is not a common edge.
Every key is therefore C98-covered and the name satisfies C100-C, yet `u` is
outside `K^O_v(d)`. All three families have full union, empty total noncore
intersection, and distinct partitions and families.

Reassigning the same third family as
`u+: FV->abe,ST->acd,WR->bcd` makes `b1/u+` an overlap-bearing common edge.
Then `b0--b1--u+` covers every key even though `b0/u+` remains a member-
disjoint common edge. This proves that C101 does not decide a general
block-to-map assignment law.

### A — no locked definition has full overlap-key coverage

`D^{K-O}_{full,v}` is empty. Every name retains its required C100 overlap edge
but also contains at least one overlap-orphan exact key. Every locked name
needs at least three keys.

A's strongest case is a deliberately disclosed **rupture form**. It has a
whole-roster foil that replaces its entire family, while comparisons preserving
one of its motifs also retain at least one Soul map. That can make an alien or
threshold simulacrum distinctive. Requiring it in every name, however, creates
a three-form comparison burden and strong token-key risk. Tight floors are
`6/10/60`; maximum degree is six. Supported Souls are

### B — fully covered and overlap-orphan locked definitions coexist

`D^{K-O}_{full,v}` is nonempty proper. At least one locked name gives every key
an overlap-bearing common partner, while another contains an overlap-orphan
key.

B is the strongest gameplay countercase if rupture is a visible,
independently valuable archetype reserved for selected names. Its costs are a
hidden behavior subtype, a promise not inferable from stable geometry, and
five locked keys across two names at the floor. Tight floors are `7/12/72`;
maximum degree is six. Supported Souls are `{6,9,10,12,14,15}`.

### C — every locked definition has full overlap-key coverage — recommended

`D^{K-O}_{full,v}=D^{U=}_{locked,v}`. For every locked definition and completed
exact key, some same-name partner changes every Soul's assigned map while
preserving and reassigning at least one exact family member. Other comparisons
may still replace the whole family.

C closes C100's one-showcase-pair loophole and gives every authored simulacrum
direct access to the strongest current **Souls and Simulacra** comparison. It
does not homogenize every edge. Costs are a per-key authoring/validation
matrix, nominal-partner access and balance risk, and the fact that four-key
names can still split into disconnected overlap-edge components. Tight floors
remain `6/9/54`; maximum degree is six. Supported Souls remain
`{6,8,9,10,12,14,15}`.

qualifying edge in a two-key name covers both vertices. The inherited outside-
locked base has five definitions and seven keys. A adds one three-key locked
name; B adds distinct two-key fully covered and three-key nonfully covered

The orphan construction extends over profiles `(2,2,2)`, `(3,3,3)`,
`(5,3,2)`, `(4,4,4)`, `(7,5,2)`, and `(5,5,5)` for six, nine, ten, twelve,

families overlaps. Every C98 common edge is therefore overlap-bearing, and
C98's key coverage puts every eight-Soul key in `K^O_v(d)`. A/B cannot support
that roster.

At one fixed exact-key/Soul-roster relationship, inherited fit remains
**direct**. Each qualifying same-Soul relation is **partial**, and the shared-
member family relation is **partial**: exact membership continues while its
carrier block changes. A/B/C's key-coverage topologies are **aggregate**. C is
recommended because every key participates in that partial relation, not
because catalog-wide incidence makes it direct.
