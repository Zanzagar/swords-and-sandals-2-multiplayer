### A — no name has a one-history-complete artifact

The set is empty. Every form of every artifact has a lived foil, but no single
lawful biography contains any artifact's complete form set. This is the
strongest principled countercase: alternate destinies are real content and no
incarnation exhausts a Relic's possible identity. It can make replay and
commitment meaningful, but it prevents any owner from experiencing the whole
authored vocabulary through one persistent artifact and can disguise missing
content as mystery unless branches are visible and comparably valuable.

### B — qualifying and wholly fragmented names coexist

The set is nonempty and proper. At least one “pilgrim” name has a represented
artifact whose whole form language unfolds within one history, while at least
one “forked-fate” name has no such artifact. This can be a strong explicit lore
taxonomy; otherwise it creates another hidden premium-name class. A positive
name may still qualify through one showcase copy.

### C — every name has a one-history-complete artifact — recommended

`D^{exists-Id-1H-allK}_{locked,v}=D^{U=}_{locked,v}`. Every locked name has at
least one represented artifact whose complete form set is materially realized
within one qualifying history. This gives every name one continuing biography
capable of expressing its whole form language while leaving the later all-copy
question open. Costs are whole-biography authoring and validation, pressure
toward completionist “grand tour” histories, and a token-carrier loophole if
one tiny, rare, forced, or hidden copy alone certifies a rich name.

## Frontier discipline

Common-history coherence is the next gameplay atom, not lived-graph
connectedness. A connected union path can stitch edges from mutually
incompatible histories and need not describe any player's journey. Conversely,
one history can contain all forms while the aligned graph remains two
disconnected dyads. Connectedness is an independent structural-hygiene sibling.

Exact state and evaluation-boundary semantics are prerequisites before causal
or agency cards, not this co-occurrence classifier. C129 selects no all-copy
one-history coverage, graph connectedness, direct transition, order, cause,
reversibility, choice, repeatability, disclosure, practical frequency, value,
power, release, or implementation. If C is selected, recompute the frontier;
the likely dependent next rung is whether every represented artifact is one-
history-complete, but do not preselect it.
