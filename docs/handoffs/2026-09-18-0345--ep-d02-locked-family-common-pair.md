C96-A says:

`does there exist one pair that changes every Soul?`

A two-key C96-A name is automatically common-pair-positive. Three or more keys

```text
COMMON-PAIR VEIL-LIKE WITNESS — degree 5, profile (4,2)
L0  F,S,V,W->{a,b}   T,R->{c,d}
L1  F,S,V,W->{a,c}   T,R->{b,d}
The sole pair changes every Soul.

STITCHED-ONLY SHROUD-LIKE WITNESS — degree 6, profile (2,2,2)
K0  F,S->{a,b,c}   V,W->{a,b,d}   T,R->{c,d,e}

K2  V,T->{a,b,d}   S,W->{a,c,d}   F,R->{b,c,e}
```

families. It therefore remains union-locked and C95-C pair-complete while its

### A — no locked definition has a common pair

`D^{W-S}_{locked,v}` is empty. C96-A coverage is stitched across at least three
keys in every name. Every comparison changes at least one Soul and retains at
least one other Soul's exact noncore map.

A's strongest case is braided continuity: every transition keeps a witness
Soul anchored while later comparisons rotate the anchor. Its costs are a
triangular comparison surface, no single whole-roster contrast, and a loophole
in which the player's Soul changes only on an obscure pair. Tight floors are
`6/10/60`; tight maximum degree is six.

### B — common-pair and stitched-only names coexist

`D^{W-S}_{locked,v}` is a nonempty proper subset. At least one name contains an
all-Soul-changing pair and another covers every Soul only across multiple
comparisons.

B supports two distinct fantasies, but adds a hidden behavioral subtype and
one-specimen token risk. Use two-key Veil beside three-key Shroud. Tight floors
are `7/12/72`; tight maximum degree is six.

### C — every locked definition has a common pair — recommended

`D^{W-S}_{locked,v}=D^{U=}_{locked,v}`. Every locked name contains at least one
pair on which every supported Soul changes. Other pairs may still retain
selected Soul maps.

C gives each name one coherent, previewable whole-roster simulacrum contrast
and prevents C96-A from becoming a scavenger hunt across forms. It preserves
Soul identity, definition, sole core, palette, and exact union as continuity.
Its costs are a common-pair authoring/proof burden for every name and loss of
purely stitched designs. Tight floors remain `6/9/54`; tight maximum degree is
five, with degree six legal.

A/B/C are exhaustive because the common-pair-positive subset is empty,
nonempty proper, or the whole nonempty locked class. All branches preserve
Soul counts `{6,8,9,10,12,14,15}`. Fixed-key fit remains direct. Each changed
same-Soul/same-definition cross-key relation is partial; the prevalence
topologies are aggregate. C is recommended for coherent participation and
**Souls and Simulacra** legibility, not a stronger direct doctrinal rating.
A's witness Soul is the strongest thematic countercase.
