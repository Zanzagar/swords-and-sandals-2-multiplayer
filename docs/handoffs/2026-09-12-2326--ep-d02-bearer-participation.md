## Sole active choice

C3c.30 asks which semantic dimensions a Relic's combatant-source grammar may
address. An action-basis predicate matches canonical action semantics and is
invariant at that basis gate to an otherwise-eligible provenance-only
substitution. A build-basis predicate may distinguish later-canonical configured
build provenance while action semantics remain fixed. Every candidate source
still obeys its own legality, accounting, and payment rules.

- **A — action-basis only:** every source-position basis predicate addresses
  action semantics. A reachable, materially functional, bearer-compliant weave
  and reachable isolation counterfactuals must prove action sensitivity and
  provenance invariance. This is the clearest action language but can reduce
  the Relic to a generic rotation tracker.
- **B — build-basis only:** every basis predicate addresses later-eligible,
  legally configured, already-accounted build semantics/provenance. A reachable
  functional witness must change solely under an otherwise-eligible provenance
  substitution with action semantics fixed. This creates strong collection
  identity but approaches a passive set bonus or gear tax.
- **C, recommended — both materially supported:** under the later composition
  rule, the catalog must contain reachable material witnesses for action
  sensitivity and build-provenance sensitivity. That later rule decides whether
  the dimensions occupy separate entries, a conjunctive entry, or both. This
  offers the broadest buildcraft but costs taxonomy/UI/test complexity and
  creates a double-view risk.

One Technique may expose candidate action and build views, but C3c.30 creates no
source atom and permits no double count. Exact family membership, provenance
key, action phase, static-versus-runtime evidence, inactive/dormant visibility,
atomization, exact arity, same-entry composition, reuse, Team Tactic/environment
admission, grammar, payoff, compatibility, horizons, persistence, validation,
and release remain later. An owner-written replacement may reopen C3c.2 by
adding a third disclosed basis.
