A current-state continuity episode is the maximal authoritative interval in
which one prospectively versioned state-form instance remains operatively
present for one source role. Establishment opens it; genuine clear, expiry,
consumption, replacement, transfer, or encounter termination ends it.

A qualifying revision is a committed canonical transition inside that interval
which leaves the same episode continuously operative, changes player-semantic
state content under a reachable held-fixed comparison, can independently alter
an effective `S` predicate, legal continuation, or authoritative outcome, and
remains material when root identity, Relic matching, payoff, and claim
bookkeeping are projected out. No-op writes, fields, aliases, caches,
timestamps, packets, logs, animations, callbacks, resolver microsteps, and
representation churn do not qualify. A clear/rewrite without an authoritative
state-absent cut cannot counterfeit termination; genuine termination plus
re-establishment is a new episode.
