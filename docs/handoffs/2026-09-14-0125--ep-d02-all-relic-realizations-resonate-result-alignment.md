## Sole active choice

For one fixed legal operative input context `h`, `Y_h` contains reachable
semantic final-input resolution states after Relic, Bound-Soul resonance, and
optional input-Charm composition but before payoff mapping. Raw UI pixels,
strings, IDs, unreachable signatures, and internal rows do not create semantic
states.

- `~L` is player-semantic equivalence: two states are equivalent when the
  player contract treats them as one declared relationship-result identity. A
  distinction must be truthfully learnable, but this does not choose layout or
  live progress presentation.
- `~C` is pre-payoff consumer equivalence: two states are equivalent when the
  consumer must treat them as the same input result and no authorized
  downstream mechanic may branch on the difference. A strict distinction needs
  a reachable material transition under at least one later-legal payoff mapping;
  an unused key or slot does not count.

Both relations are extensional over the complete reachable domain. A strict
distinction needs a reachable witness; incomplete analysis is unresolved.

- **A, recommended — exact alignment everywhere:** `~L=~C` in every operative
  context. `P` and `Q` may both collapse to one `P or Q` result, or both
  contracts may preserve them as two results. This does not decide which count;
  it ensures no decorative result that mechanics erase and no hidden consumer
  branch. It guarantees honest identity, not fun by itself; content, payoff,
  pacing, arbitration, and presentation still determine fun.
- **B — player-refined, consumer-aggregated somewhere:** `~L` is a subset of
  `~C` everywhere and strictly finer somewhere. The player receives at least
  one distinction that collapses before payoff consumption, while no consumer
  distinction is hidden. This supports separately celebrated mastery or
  explanation, but risks UI busywork that looks mechanically meaningful when
  it is not.
- **C — the consumer outruns the player somewhere:** some reachable states are
  equivalent under `~L` but distinct under `~C`. The player sees one success
  while a later mechanic materially distinguishes how it occurred. This permits
  mystery but creates hidden optimization and apparent inconsistency. Uniformly
  consumer-finer and incomparable/mixed subcases remain dependent if selected.

A/B/C are exhaustive: either a consumer distinction is hidden, giving C, or
`~L` is a subset of `~C` everywhere; then equality everywhere gives A and a
strict player refinement gives B. One-result contexts trivially satisfy local
alignment. B derives at least two player-result classes somewhere and C at
least two consumer classes; exact breadth and aligned-channel multiplicity
remain later.
