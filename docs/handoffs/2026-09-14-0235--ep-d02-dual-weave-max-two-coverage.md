## Corrected frontier result

Co-result coverage is the next prerequisite before a stable behavioral mapping
key. A read-only audit found that C3c.43's `R_d+` domain cannot be used
unqualified: it includes predicate-operative realizations, while C3c.45 allows
an operative context to have no positive receipt after result projection and
leaves those receipt-silent cases to later operability/content design.

Without a restricted denominator, a wholly receipt-silent realization could
falsely satisfy a “focused-only” complement. C3c.47 therefore classifies only
receipt-bearing realizations. Receipt-silent operative realizations and
definitions remain explicitly outside the card and cannot satisfy any negative
coverage witness.

After that repair, coverage precedes mapping because it determines which
mapping levels can remain material. Mapping must later be expressed as a
behavioral substitution invariant rather than a storage namespace.

## Sole active choice

For fixed version `v` and supported definition `d`, retain C3c.43's operative
realizations `R_{d,v}+` and define:

- `B_{d,v}` contains exactly those realizations with at least one legal fixed
  anchored context in `H+`; these are receipt-bearing. Definitions represented
  in that domain form `D_{B,v}`.
- `K_{d,v}` contains those `B_{d,v}` realizations with at least one legal fixed
  context and reachable state emitting exactly two receipts. These are
  dual-weave-capable.

Use tagged definition/realization unions `B_v*` and `K_v*`. Selected C3c.45-C
and C3c.46-A make `D_{B,v}` and `K_v*` nonempty. A receipt-bearing realization
is focused-only only after complete analysis of every legal anchored positive
context and every reachable state proves width at most one. Failure to find a
double or incomplete analysis is unresolved. The one no-co-result context from
C3c.45-C does not prove a focused-only realization because that same
realization may dual elsewhere.

- **A, recommended — every receipt-bearing realization has dual access:**
  `K_v*=B_v*`. Every receipt-bearing realization has at least one legal dual
  context, although its other Soul/Charm/build/cell contexts may remain
  focused. This avoids premium-instance and premium-definition hierarchies and
  makes optimization able to reach the mastery feature everywhere. Its cost is
  a universal feature/authoring tax and possible homogenization.
- **B — every represented definition has access, but realizations mix:**
  `K_v*` is a proper subset of `B_v*`, while every represented definition has
  nonempty `K_{d,v}`. At least one definition therefore has both a capable and
  proven focused-only receipt-bearing realization. This supports personal
  artifact variance but creates the strongest premium-roll risk under
  one-instance ownership, duplicate substitution, permanence, and no transfer.
- **C — only some represented definitions have dual access:** at least one
  represented definition has nonempty `K_{d,v}` and at least one distinct
  represented definition has empty `K_{d,v}`. This creates clear named focused
  and dual archetypes, but dual-capable definitions can become a premium class;
  capable definitions may still hide B-like realization variance until a later
  choice.

Illustrative A lets exact Ashen `R17/state0/boundaryA` find a dual context under
Firebound plus Ember and exact Dreamglass `R44/state1/boundaryB` find a
different one under Stonebound plus an empty input lane. Other contexts remain
focused. Illustrative B makes receipt-bearing Ashen `R17` capable while
alternate Ashen `R44` is proven focused-only, although every definition has
some capable realization. Illustrative C gives some Dreamglass realization a
dual context while every receipt-bearing Ashen realization is focused-only.
Names and mappings are not selected.

A/B/C are exhaustive over the receipt-bearing tagged domain. A applies if
coverage is universal. If not, B applies when every represented definition has
some capability; otherwise global nonempty capability and one empty represented
definition give C. B derives a same-definition capable/focused-only pair; C
derives at least two represented definitions.

This card selects only binary access to some dual context. It does not select
ease, frequency, power, Charm-free access, every Soul/Charm participation,
exact Soul/Charm/source/cell mapping or stability, transformations, cross-cell
footprints, overlap/reuse, input or payoff arbitration, UI, evolution,
acquisition, persistence/migration, validation, release, or implementation.
