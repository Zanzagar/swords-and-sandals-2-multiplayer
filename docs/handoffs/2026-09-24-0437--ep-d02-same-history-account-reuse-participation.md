mean equality of raw storage ID, wording, localization, reference asset,

`I^{pre-int}_v(k,w,d)={xi : there exist pi_P,pi_R with
(xi,pi_P,pi_R) in Gamma^{pre-int}_v(w,d)}`.

`P^{pre-reuse}_v(k,w)={{d_i,d_j} subseteq D^{joint}(w) :

### A — every dual tag has a same-history reused account — recommended

`E^{pre-reuse}_v=D^{dual}_v`. Every dual tag has at least one exact legal
history and one pair of distinct joint opportunities whose qualifying

and pairs may remain reuse-negative.

## Proof, anti-laundering, floors, and deferrals

A/B/C are exhaustive. C169-A makes `W^{2j}_v(k)` nonempty for every dual tag,
and C168-A makes each joint opportunity's `I^{pre-int}` nonempty. A tag has a
same-history opportunity pair with a nonempty account-identity intersection
or it does not. `E^{pre-reuse}_v` is the whole dual domain, empty, or nonempty
proper.

A requires, for every dual tag, one exact `w,d_i,d_j,xi` and a separate
`Gamma^{pre-int}` witness at each opportunity proving that the same `xi`
qualifies at both. B requires exhaustive enumeration of every multi-
opportunity history, unordered opportunity pair, canonical account identity,
and qualifying P/R mapping, proving every intersection empty. C combines one
A-style positive tag with one distinct exhaustively reuse-negative tag.
Incomplete enumeration remains unresolved.

The same reference asset, page, panel, heading, string, icon, narrator,
template, UI position, or equivalent prose does not prove reuse. An alias or
unversioned ID cannot hide changed causal scope or provenance. The same
account merely present at both choices does not count unless it independently
satisfies `Gamma^{pre-int}` at both; supplying P at one choice and R at the
other cannot be stitched. Two views at one opportunity, branch-exclusive
choices, different histories, reloads, replays, player memory, generic
primers, and neutral baselines cannot manufacture a reuse edge. Conversely,
localization, layout, linked-view, and rendering differences do not defeat
reuse when the exact canonical identity is unchanged and truthful at both.
