## Selected direction

In addition to C3c.36-C's cross-form witness, the completed catalog requires
distinct reachable, materially functional, bearer-compliant `E_H` and `E_S`
witness entries. This derives at least three distinct final effective entries:

- `E_H`: every independently attributable position is history-only;
- `E_S`: every independently attributable position is state-only; and
- `E_X`: distinct history-only and state-only positions coexist.

This does not require three Relic definitions or map any temporal type to pure-

## Sole active choice

C3c.31-A makes every final `E+` entry pure action or pure build and requires
both basis classes somewhere. For each temporal type `t` in `{E_H,E_S,E_X}`,
call it **basis-dual** only when the catalog contains distinct reachable,
materially functional, bearer-compliant pure-action and pure-build entries of
that temporal type. This is catalog support, not one hybrid entry, a paired
definition, or a dual-sensitive position.

- **A — no temporal type is basis-dual:** each of `E_H`, `E_S`, and `E_X`
  appears in exactly one basis class. At least one must be action-only and at
  least one build-only; all-action/all-build mappings violate C3c.31-A. This
  retains the minimum of three entries but later requires one of six nonconstant
  row assignments. It is compact, but hard-wires temporal playstyles to bases
  that may feel arbitrary.
- **B, recommended — every temporal type is basis-dual:** require separate
  pure-action and pure-build witnesses for each of `E_H`, `E_S`, and `E_X`.
  This derives at least six distinct final entries and closes the temporal-to-
  basis support branch. It keeps *when evidence matters* independent from *what
  the recipe recognizes*, while entries remain single-basis and positions
  remain single-form. The cost is the largest grid and serious filler/reskin
  pressure; the six cells must be materially functional but need not have
  pairwise-distinct mechanics, identities, or payoffs.
- **C — some but not all temporal types are basis-dual:** one or two temporal
  types have both action/build witnesses and the rest have one basis each. One
  dual type derives at least four entries; two derive at least five. This avoids
  a mandatory six-cell grid but adds choices for dual count, membership, and
  every remaining singleton basis, with a risk of arbitrary exceptions.

A/B/C exhaust whether the basis-dual temporal-type set is empty, universal, or
a nonempty proper subset. Under A and C, their row mappings remain later. Under
B all six temporal/basis support cells are required, but their entries need not
share Relics, source families, predicate structures, or payoffs.

Every required cell needs its applicable action/build and history/state
isolation evidence. All-bearer and bearer-plus-ally witnesses remain catalog-
wide rather than repeating per cell. Stable identity mapping, paired-content
symmetry, subtypes, phase/window, operators/order, arity/roles,
atoms/distinctness/reuse, origin mapping, basis-neutral admission, extra
coupling, payoff, UI, compatibility, horizons, persistence, validation, and
release remain open.
