## Frontier audit

Let

`D^mu_{stable-mixed,v}=D^mixed_v \ D^mu_{var,v}`

and, for profile multiplicities `m_r`, define

`D^repeat_v={d in D^mu_{stable-mixed,v}:some r>=2 has m_r(d)>=2}`.

Holding one named-Soul role map fixed, the number of compatible exact
partitions is

`product_r ((r*m_r)! / ((r!)^m_r * m_r!))`.

C75-A makes every group size at least two, so this exceeds one exactly when
some size occurs in at least two blocks. A mixed `(4,2)` role map fixes its
partition; `(4,2,2)` permits the two size-two blocks to regroup members.
Repeated-size support is therefore the exact missing eligibility projection
before actual partner variation. It does not prove that two keys realize it.

Equal-size affinity definitions are forced support-positive and stay outside
C78, then return in the later actual-partner denominator. Role-variable mixed
definitions are already partition-variable and also stay outside that later
stability denominator, but they may still contain additional equal-role,
different-partner key pairs. C78 does not exhaust that finer behavior. Core-
label continuity remains a separate ready sibling.

## Active frontier

- **A, recommended — no stable mixed definition has repeated-size support:**
  `D^repeat_v` is empty. This produces three clear dialects: equal-size names
  retain partner-rewiring potential, stable mixed names become fixed asymmetric
  covenants, and role-variable mixed names are morphic. It is neutral/
  protective at C78's boundary and preserves inherited direct fixed-key fit.
  The cost is losing the same-role/different-companion fantasy inside stable
  mixed names.
- **B — some stable mixed definitions have repeated-size support:**
  `D^repeat_v` is nonempty proper. Fixed covenants and “braided covenants”
  coexist. The extra dialect can matter if predicates and UI make named peers
  meaningful, but forces at least two stable mixed names, eight Souls, and
  degree-four affinity support. C78's support fit remains neutral and catalog
  coexistence aggregate; one unused `(4,2,2)` name can tokenize B.
- **C — every stable mixed definition has repeated-size support:**
  `D^repeat_v=D^mu_{stable-mixed,v}`. Every stable mixed name can potentially
  regroup peers while preserving size roles. This maximizes latent relational
  authoring but removes the fixed-covenant dialect, requires at least eight
  Souls and degree four, blurs the contrast with equal-size names, and still
  allows one-key names whose partners never actually change.
