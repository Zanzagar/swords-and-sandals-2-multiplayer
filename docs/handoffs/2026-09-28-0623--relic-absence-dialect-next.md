## Present exactly this next

RCS-03A-C partitions nonempty `D_mut` into nonempty disjoint state-supporting
`D_S` and boundary-supporting `D_B`. State Relics transform a lineage-carried
remembered condition at fixed evaluation boundary. Boundary Relics transform a
lineage-carried remembered evaluation-boundary attunement at fixed state. One
definition never transforms both axes.

Define `D_Z^S=D_Z ∩ D_S` and `D_Z^B=D_Z ∩ D_B`. D1Z-C makes
`D_Z` nonempty proper. Therefore both intersections cannot be empty, and the
three remaining incidence patterns are exhaustive:

- **A — state-dialect support only.** `D_Z^S` is nonempty and `D_Z^B` is
  empty. **“Absence changes what the Relic bears.”** A Hollow Ashen in `Mercy`

  `Oath` may commit `{}` and propose `ECHO ATTUNEMENT`; a state Ashen has no Z
  proposal. This has the sharpest relation-through-absence fantasy, but the
  most opacity, best-listening-point, snapshot, self-retune-loop, and ally-
  disruption risk.

  retained.** Both intersections are nonempty while `D_Z` remains globally

  coordinates. Cost: two policy surfaces must each be legible, material,
  non-dominated, and anti-exploit; token representation fails.
