## Semantic boundary

Let `C_v^D` be the class made nonempty by B1-C whose controlled-form projection
is exactly `{D}` under B2-A. Let `J_v^D` contain its contracts with at least one
reachable genuine-conflict opportunity classified `I`. Universal `I` support
means every contract has **at least one** such opportunity; every member still
has at least one valid direct opportunity.

Classify lawful semantic controls, not UI deliveries or execution traces. A
`D` witness requires two lawful live participant-control vectors that differ
only in one current-result coordinate, name distinct still-valid targets, and
produce completed laws committing their respective targets.

- Invalid or stale choices, duplicate prompts, confirmation, one mandatory
  default, timeout, disconnect, unavailable-holder handling, or fallback
  execution supplies neither a second direct arm nor an authored `I` witness.
- An advance-bound material rule, resolver delegate, veto, filter, vote, or
  ranking is `P`, not `D`, and would contradict B2-A's exact-`{D}` class.
- If the only UI control is `override -> DEFIANCE` while immutable law defaults
  to `MERCY`, timeout/default is not a participant arm. The opportunity is
  neither `D` nor `I`; add an explicit live `choose MERCY` arm, which makes a
  direct target choice, or reopen the form design.
- One valid target before cohort freeze is not genuine conflict. Post-freeze
  invalidation is a stale-action/fault case, not `I`.

## Highest-value next step

Ask for B2I A, B, C, or an explicit replacement:

- **A — direct specialists ask at every genuine conflict.** Every contract is
  exact `{D}`. Soul-answered Ashen always presents live `MERCY` and `DEFIANCE`
  target arms. This is maximally immediate and simple, but may create prompt
  fatigue, target shopping, quarterbacking, latency/default harm, and an
  always-controllable option premium.
- **B — every direct specialist also has an authored self-willed context.
  Recommended.** Every contract is exact `{I,D}`. Ashen asks ordinarily, but
  its prospectively disclosed **Last Witness** context invokes immutable
  covenant without participant result control: “sometimes it asks the Soul;
  in its sealed context, it answers for itself.” This prevents direct choice
  from being literally universal and leaves later cadence authoring room
  without adding another action language, but a weak boundary can feel like
  arbitrary denial.
- **C — exact `{D}` and `{I,D}` direct specialists coexist.** Some always ask;
  others have self-willed contexts. This maximizes item-specific identity and
  also the hidden-tier risk that exact `{D}` becomes the premium caste.

Recommend **B with A as the qualified practical agency/clarity fallback**.
This intentionally differs from B1I-A: policy can bind in advance, whereas
direct choice is live by definition. Exact `{D}` therefore puts a live target
decision at every genuine conflict. B establishes mixed support and lets later
authoring pursue a clear rhythm of Soul choice and Relic self-will without
granting extra action breadth; B2I itself fixes no frequency, and one rare or
contrived token `I` witness is insufficient.

Fire A only when both conditions hold: after the best one-sentence rule,
forecast, and Rite disclosure, B's `I/D` boundary still feels like arbitrary
denial or can be met only by token contexts; and exact-`{D}` testing shows
naturally infrequent, bounded, non-solved prompts, acceptable multiplayer and
default handling, and no unavoidable dominance over policy or intrinsic
specialists. C is a narrow identity exception, not the fallback.

Each `D` opportunity can directly express *Achintya Bheda Abheda* when Soul
choice and immutable Relic grammar are separately indispensable. An `I`
opportunity preserves Relic distinction but has no participant control, so
the B profile's alternation is only a partial cross-context fit. No answer
earns scalar compensation or a “two modes” bonus; equal optimized Legendary
ceilings remain later proof.
