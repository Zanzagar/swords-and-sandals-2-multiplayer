- the **exact class** repeats one identical final child occurrence across that

- the **related-only residual** repeats no final child inside that ledger pair,

Different pairs inside one three-Relic state classify independently. If
Aster/Borel share exact `Heat h` while Borel/Cyra overlap only through distinct
